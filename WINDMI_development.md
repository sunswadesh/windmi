# WINDMI Model: Development and Maintenance

This document outlines the development history and maintenance of the WINDMI (solar Wind Magnetosphere Ionosphere) model, based on information from the `README.md` file.

## Original Developers

The WINDMI model was originally developed by a team at the Institute for Fusion Studies, Department of Physics, University of Texas at Austin. The key developers credited are:

*   W. Horton
*   M. L. Mays
*   E. Spencer
*   I. Doxas

Their work laid the foundation for the model's structure, governing equations, and initial applications.

## Maintenance and Modifications

The model has since been maintained and further modified by:

*   Swadesh Patra

Swadesh Patra's contributions include overseeing the model's upkeep and implementing various adaptations and versions, ensuring its continued relevance and development. These adaptations, detailed elsewhere, include enhancements like variable ionospheric conductivity and the prediction of the SymH index.
